<p>Here’s an obscure life hack.</p><p>If (like me) you:</p><ul><li><p>Don’t like going to bed, due to it conflicting with keeping on doing things</p></li><li><p>When in bed, wish you had something more engaging and enjoyable to do instead of just lying there waiting—for instance, playing a very compelling computer game until you get tired</p></li><li><p>Do not in fact reliably get tired from playing a very compelling computer game</p></li><li><p>Like puzzles</p></li></ul><p>..then a solution that has worked surprisingly well for me before (and I <a href="https://worldspiritsockpuppet.com/2021/03/07/sleep-math-red-clay-blue-clay.html">wrote about previously</a>) is having hard math puzzles handy to think about as I go to sleep. Somehow thinking about math at the bounds of my limited ability to imagine does make me sleepy. And is also quite compelling.</p>

<!--ex-->

<p>I haven’t done this for a while, I think due to disorganization and losing track of the puzzles I had, and not having a great source of new ones. But I have recently reinstated this in a nice format.</p><p>I think ideally you want this to involve no preparation at all at bed time, and no going on your computer. So you want a nice supply of the right kind of puzzles in a physical place near your bed. So I sorted through puzzles and found <a href="https://docs.google.com/document/d/1rRdgJxA5M9uYBH0vZaTuuTq1dpnSv2nFRN8TQw4es9c/edit?tab=t.0">a bunch</a> of good-looking ones, printed them out, cut them up, folded them into pleasing parcels, and put them in my ex-housemate’s ex-teapot. Now if I’m ready for bed, I can draw a tiny package, and open something actually fun and helpful to think about in bed.</p>
